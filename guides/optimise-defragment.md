# Optimise and defragment a drive

Open Windows' Optimise Drives tool and set how often it runs.

**When you'd use it:** a hard drive (HDD) has slowed down because files are fragmented, or you want to check the drive's optimisation schedule.

## Steps
1. Right-click the **Start** button and open **Disk Management**.
2. Find the drive, in this case **C:**, right-click it and choose **Properties**.
3. Open the **Tools** tab. Under **Optimise and defragment drive**, click **Optimise**.
4. The **Optimise Drives** window opens. From here you can:
   - select the drive and click **Optimise**, or
   - change the settings, such as how often it runs and which drives are included.

## What I learned
- Windows already optimises drives on a schedule, and this window is where you check or change it.
- The tool picks the right method for the drive type: it defragments HDDs and runs TRIM on SSDs. SSDs shouldn't be defragmented.
