# Driver updates (including graphics)

Update device drivers through Device Manager and the GPU vendor's app.

**When you'd use it:** a device isn't working properly (e.g. Bluetooth or audio), or graphics performance is poor or unstable.

## Part 1: Device Manager
1. Open **Device Manager**, from Windows search or by right-clicking the Start button.
2. Find the device to update. I showed my Bluetooth and my speaker.
3. Right-click the device and choose **Update driver**.
4. Choose one of the two options:
   - **Search automatically for drivers:** Windows finds and installs one for you.
   - **Browse my computer for drivers:** for when you already have the driver file.
5. I chose **Search automatically**. Windows reported the best driver was already installed.

## Part 2: Graphics driver (NVIDIA)
Graphics drivers come from the card's vendor, so this depends on the PC: NVIDIA, AMD or Intel.

1. Download the vendor's software from their website. Mine is the **NVIDIA app**.
2. Open the app and go to **Drivers**.
3. Click **Install** on the latest driver, and **Yes** at the confirmation prompt.
4. The driver starts downloading and installing. This takes a while, so the video ends here, with an explanation of what happens once it completes.

## What I learned
- Device Manager handles most hardware, but graphics drivers are best updated through the vendor's own app.
- Keeping drivers up to date helps **performance and stability**. Out-of-date drivers can leave security holes that malware can exploit.
